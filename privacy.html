Real One Quiz

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy covers the Real One Quiz website and the Real One Quiz Stats tool. The tool is used only by the channel operator, and only with the channel's own accounts.

Services used

The tool uses YouTube API Services and the Meta platform APIs for Facebook and Instagram. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Meta's is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed and stored

  • Statistics of the channel's own videos and accounts, such as views, reactions, comment counts, saves and follower counts.
  • Identifiers and metadata of the channel's own videos, such as IDs, titles and publication times.
  • Access tokens for the channel's own accounts.

The tool does not collect names, messages, comment text or other personal data of viewers. Statistics are used in aggregated form only.

How data is used

The data is used to measure the performance of the channel's own videos and to upload and schedule its own videos. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

Storage and sharing

Data and tokens are stored on the operator's own computer. Data is not sold. It is not shared with anyone, except that videos and their texts are sent to the platforms where they are published.

Website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising and contains no third-party content. Its hosting provider, GitHub, may process technical request data such as IP addresses.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept while the channel operates. To request deletion, see Data Deletion.

Revoking access

Access granted to the tool on a Google account can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Contact

Privacy questions and complaints: realonequiz@gmail.com
