Data Deletion

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The tool stores data only about the channel's own accounts. It stores no personal data of viewers.

To request deletion of any data, email realonequiz@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and describe the data concerned. Requests are answered within 30 days.

Access can also be removed directly: on the Google security settings page, or in Facebook under Settings, Business Integrations.
