Real One Quiz

Real One Quiz is a short video quiz channel. Each video shows a numbered grid of AI-generated portraits, and viewers try to find the one that looks like the real person. All videos are made with AI and are disclosed as such.

Watch on YouTube and TikTok. The channel is also on Facebook and Instagram.

Real One Quiz Stats

Real One Quiz Stats is a private tool used only by the channel operator. It works only with the channel's own YouTube, Facebook and Instagram accounts. It does two things:

  • It reads view and engagement statistics of the channel's own videos, to measure which videos work.
  • It uploads and schedules the channel's own approved videos.

The tool is not offered to other people. It does not access anyone else's account.
